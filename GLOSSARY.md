# Cadence

Cadence is an online course platform where Students buy and follow Courses that Instructors create and publish.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A user who is enrolled in a Course and follows it.
_Avoid_: Learner, member, user (in user-facing text)

**Buyer**:
The person who paid for a Purchase. For a team Purchase, this is often not one of the Students.
_Avoid_: Purchaser, spender, customer

**Instructor**:
A user who creates Courses and answers Comments on them.
_Avoid_: Teacher, creator

**Admin**:
A user who runs the whole platform, across all Courses and users.
_Avoid_: Administrator, staff (for this role alone)

**Staff**:
Instructors and Admins together: the people who may answer Comments on behalf of Cadence.
_Avoid_: Moderator, "instructor or admin"

**Team**:
The group of people who share the Seats from one team Purchase.
_Avoid_: Organization, group, company

**Team owner**:
The person who owns a Team's Seats and hands out its Coupons. Usually the Buyer of the team Purchase.
_Avoid_: Team admin, team manager, team lead

### Course content

**Course**:
A complete program on one subject, made up of Modules and sold as a whole.
_Avoid_: Class, program, training

**Module**:
An ordered group of Lessons within a Course.
_Avoid_: Section, chapter, unit

**Lesson**:
One unit of teaching inside a Module, with text, a video, or both.
_Avoid_: Lecture, video, episode

**Category**:
The subject area a Course is filed under.
_Avoid_: Topic, tag, genre

**Draft / Published / Archived**:
The states of a Course: still being built and hidden, open to Students, or withdrawn from sale.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, live, hidden

**Rating**:
A Student's score for a Course, from one to five.
_Avoid_: Review, stars

### Quizzes

**Quiz**:
A set of Questions attached to a Lesson that checks what the Student has learned.
_Avoid_: Test, exam, assessment

**Question**:
One item in a Quiz that the Student answers.
_Avoid_: Using "question" for a Comment

**Option**:
One of the possible answers to a Question, of which one or more are correct.
_Avoid_: Choice, answer (for the possibilities offered)

**Attempt**:
One time a Student takes a Quiz, from start to submitted answers.
_Avoid_: Try, go, submission

**Passing score**:
The share of correct answers a Student needs in an Attempt to pass the Quiz.
_Avoid_: Threshold, pass mark

**Passed / Failed**:
Whether an Attempt reached the Passing score.
_Avoid_: Succeeded, got

### Progress

**Not started / In progress / Completed**:
The states of a Student's progress through a Lesson.
_Avoid_: New, started, finished, done

**Completed**:
A Lesson or Course that a Student has gone through to the end.
_Avoid_: Finished, done

### Comments

**Comment**:
A message a Student or Staff member posts under a Lesson.
_Avoid_: Question, discussion, post

**Reply**:
A Comment that responds to another Comment.
_Avoid_: Response, answer

**Answered / Unanswered**:
Whether a Comment has at least one Reply from Staff.
_Avoid_: Resolved, open, waiting

### Buying and access

**Purchase**:
A payment for a Course, made by a Buyer.
_Avoid_: Sale, order, spend

**Buy**:
The verb for making a Purchase. Used on buttons that lead to payment.
_Avoid_: Enroll (when money changes hands), join, get started

**Enrollment**:
A Student's access to a Course. A Student enrolls by buying the Course or by redeeming a Coupon.
_Avoid_: Registration, joining

**Seat**:
One place in a Course, bought as part of a team Purchase so someone else can enroll.

**Coupon**:
What a Seat comes with: a single-use code that gives one person an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Code (on its own), voucher

**Coupon link**:
The URL that redeems a Coupon.
_Avoid_: Redemption link, link (on its own)

**Redeemed / Unredeemed**:
Whether a Coupon has been used to enroll someone.
_Avoid_: Claimed, unclaimed, used, unused

**Regional discount**:
A lower price for a Course based on the Student's country, so that it costs about the same relative to local incomes.
_Avoid_: PPP, purchasing power parity, parity discount, location-based pricing, tier

### Refunds

**Refund**:
Money paid for a Purchase that a Student asks us to give back, within the Refund window.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, cancellation, reversal

**Refund window**:
The period after a Purchase during which a Student may ask for a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cooling-off period

**Refund request**:
A Refund that the Student has asked for and that we have not yet approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, pending refund, open refund

**Requested / Approved / Declined**:
The three states of a Refund: asked for, granted, or turned down.
_Avoid_: Pending, raised, accepted, rejected, denied

### Accounts and navigation

**Log in / Log out / Sign up**:
Getting into an existing account, leaving it, and creating a new one.
_Avoid_: Sign in, sign out, register, authenticate

**Dashboard**:
The page where a Student sees their Enrollments and progress.
_Avoid_: My dashboard, home

**Settings**:
The page where a user manages their own account.

**Profile**:
The part of Settings that holds a user's name, email and bio.
_Avoid_: Details

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Chargeback" was used for a Refund request. A chargeback is a payment reversal forced through the bank, outside our control, and Cadence has no such concept. Use **Refund**.
- "Cancel" was used for asking for a Refund. In general, cancelling and refunding are different things. When the Student wants their money back, say **Refund**.
- "Enroll" was used on a button that leads to payment. Paying is **Buy**. **Enrollment** is the access that follows.
- "Admin" meant both the platform role and the person who owns a Team's Seats. The platform role is **Admin** and the team role is **Team owner**.
- "Question" meant both a Comment under a Lesson and an item in a Quiz. A Comment is a **Comment**, and **Question** is reserved for Quizzes.
- "Review" meant three things: revisiting a Course, checking a Quiz before saving it, and Staff looking at a Refund request. A Student's opinion of a Course is a **Rating**. For the other meanings, use a plain verb such as revisit, check or look at.
